We design houses for a particular plot and a particular family. Before we draw anything we spend time on the site, at different hours, to see where the light and the views are.

What is included

  • A site visit and a study of sun, views and access
  • Two or three concept options with a rough cost
  • Permit drawings and the application
  • Construction drawings and a full specification
  • Help choosing a builder, and weekly site visits until handover
