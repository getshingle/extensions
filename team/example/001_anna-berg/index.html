Anna studied architecture in Copenhagen and spent eight years designing public housing before she founded Atrium in 2012. Those years taught her that a good home does not depend on its budget, but on how carefully it is planned around the people who live in it.

She leads all of the studio’s new homes and still draws the first sketches of every project by hand, usually on the site itself.

Outside the studio she teaches a design course at the local school of architecture and spends too much time looking at old timber barns.
