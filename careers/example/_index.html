Working at Atrium

We work in one room, on a small number of projects at a time. Everyone draws, everyone visits sites and everyone talks to clients. New colleagues work next to the founders from their first week.

We keep reasonable hours. Late evenings before a deadline happen a few times a year, not every week, and they are given back as time off.

What we offer

  • A permanent contract after a three-month trial period
  • 30 days of paid holiday, plus the week between Christmas and New Year
  • Two days a week from home, where the role allows it
  • A yearly budget for courses, books and study trips
  • A bicycle or a public transport pass, paid by the studio

How we hire

  1. Send us your CV and a few projects you are proud of. A short PDF is better than a long one.
  2. A first conversation of about an hour, in the studio or by video call.
  3. A paid trial day working on a real project with the team.
  4. An answer within a week of the trial day, whatever the answer is.
