We are looking for an architect with at least four years of experience who would like to take responsibility for whole projects: new homes and renovations with building budgets between 300,000 and 1.5 million.

What you will do

  • Lead two or three projects at a time, from the first sketch to handover
  • Prepare permit and construction drawings with the team
  • Visit building sites every week and be the builder’s first contact
  • Present to clients and keep them informed about cost and time

What we are looking for

  • A degree in architecture and a license to practice, or close to getting one
  • At least four years of experience, including time on building sites
  • Confidence with a CAD or BIM program; we use Archicad and are happy to teach it
  • Clear writing and calm conversations with clients and builders

What we offer

A permanent contract, 30 days of holiday, two days a week from home and a yearly training budget. The salary for this role is 52,000 to 62,000 a year, depending on experience.
