--- title: We have moved to Harbour Lane summary: After ten years in two small rooms, the studio now has space for a model workshop and a meeting table for twelve. cover_alt: The front of the old harbour office with its tall windows and green door ---

From Monday 6 October you will find us at 12 Harbour Lane, on the ground floor of the old harbour office.

The building dates from 1928. We kept the tall windows, the terrazzo floor and the cast iron columns, and added a workshop for models and material samples at the back. There is a long table where we can lay out drawings with clients, and a bicycle rack by the door.

Our phone number and email stay the same. Come and see us, and please call ahead so one of us is sure to be in.
