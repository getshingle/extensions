--- title: Riverside House is finished summary: The family moved in this summer, two years after our first visit to a very wet and very beautiful plot by the river. ---

Riverside House is a timber home for a family of five on a plot that floods every few winters. The living rooms sit one floor up, with a covered terrace looking down the river; the ground floor is built to get wet and dry out again.

The house was built in eleven months, on budget, by a local builder we had not worked with before and would gladly work with again.

We will publish photos and drawings once the garden has grown in.
