--- title: Free planning advice on the first Thursday of the month summary: Bring your plans, photos or questions to the studio between 16:00 and 18:00. No appointment needed. ---

Many people have a question about their home that is too small for a project but too big to guess: can I add a window here, do I need a permit for this, is this wall load-bearing?

On the first Thursday of every month, from 16:00 to 18:00, one of our architects is in the studio to answer exactly these questions. It is free and you do not need to book. Bring whatever you have: photos, a sketch, the deeds or just the question.
