--- title: Seven questions to ask before you hire an architect summary: Choosing an architect is choosing who you will spend the next year with. These questions help you find out quickly if it will work. tag: Advice author: anna-berg cover_alt: A floor plan sketch on a wooden desk with a pencil and a scale ruler ---

Most people hire an architect once in their life, so it is hard to know what to ask. These are the questions we would ask ourselves if we were on your side of the table.

1. Who will actually design my project?

In many practices the person who wins the job is not the person who draws it. Ask to meet the architect who will work on your home day to day, and make sure you get along.

2. Have you built something like this before?

Not the same house, but the same kind of problem: a narrow plot, an old building, a tight budget. Ask to see the drawings and photos, and ask what they would do differently now.

3. How do you charge?

A percentage of the building cost, a fixed fee, or an hourly rate. All three are fair, as long as you know what is included and when you pay. Ask for the fee per stage, in writing.

4. What will it cost to build?

A good architect gives you an honest range early on, even if it is not what you hoped to hear. Be careful with anyone who only talks about the design and never about the budget.

5. How long will it take?

Ask for a realistic timeline for each stage, including the time the permit takes. Planning offices are rarely as fast as anyone wants.

6. Who do I call when something goes wrong on site?

Something always does. You want to know that the architect visits the site regularly and will deal with the builder on your behalf.

7. Can I talk to a past client?

Any architect worth hiring will be glad to put you in touch with someone they have worked for. Ask that person what surprised them.

We are happy to answer all seven in a first conversation, at no cost.
