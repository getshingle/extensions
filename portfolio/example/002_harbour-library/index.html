The brief

The town needed a bigger library and owned an empty warehouse on the harbour. The council asked us whether the building could be saved, and what it would cost compared with building new.

What we did

  • Kept the brick walls and the timber roof, and added a row of roof windows to bring daylight to the middle of the building
  • Placed a new floor inside the old walls for the children's library and quiet study rooms
  • Opened the ground floor towards the harbour with a café and a terrace
  • Planned the work in stages, so the old library stayed open until the move

The result

Saving the warehouse cost less than building new, and used a fraction of the material. Visits doubled in the first year, and the reading room is booked most evenings for clubs and courses.
