The brief

A furniture maker bought the old bakery on Mill Street to live above it and work below. The building was dark, cold and divided into small rooms, but the arched windows and the old oven were worth keeping.

What we did

  • Removed later walls to make one open kitchen and living room on the first floor
  • Insulated the building from the inside, so the brick front could stay as it is
  • Turned the bakery into a workshop with the original arched windows, and kept the oven as a fireplace

The result

The flat now gets sun from morning to evening, and the workshop has become a small shop on Saturdays. The street got its bakery windows back, lit again in the evening.
