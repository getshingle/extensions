The brief

The Lunds bought a beautiful, very wet plot by the river. They wanted a home for five that would feel open to the water, survive the floods that come every few winters, and stay within a budget set before the first drawing.

What we did

  • Raised the kitchen, living room and bedrooms one floor up, with a covered terrace along the river side
  • Designed the ground floor to get wet and dry out again: concrete, tiles and a workshop instead of living space
  • Used larch cladding and wood fibre insulation, sourced within a hundred kilometres
  • Worked with a local builder from the first sketch, so the budget was checked at every step

The result

The house was built in eleven months and finished on budget. It uses a third of the energy of the family's old flat, and in the first flood the ground floor was clean and back in use within a week.
